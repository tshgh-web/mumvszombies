CAMERA & VIDEO

MUM VS ZOMBIES uses your device camera solely to detect body pose for gameplay. All processing happens entirely on your own device using the MediaPipe library. No video, images, or camera data is ever transmitted to any server, stored, or shared with any third party. Your camera feed never leaves your browser.

LEADERBOARD DATA

If you choose to submit a score to the global leaderboard, only your chosen display name and numerical score are stored. No email, device ID, IP address, or personal information is collected or linked to your score entry. Leaderboard data is stored on JSONBin.io, a third-party service.

The game saves your preferences (skin, weapon, backdrop, local high scores, unlock progress) in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device only and is never transmitted.
